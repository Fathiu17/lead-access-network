## The network the African Free Fire scene has been waiting for

**LEAD ACCESS Network (LAN)** is officially live. We exist for one reason: to give the Free Fire esports community across Africa a single, reliable, verified source of information.

For too long, roster moves have been rumoured before they were confirmed. Tournament brackets have appeared minutes before lock. Results have been argued about in group chats instead of published. That ends now.

### What we publish

- **News** — verified announcements from organisers, teams and publishers.
- **Rosters** — confirmed signings, transfers, stand-ins and roster locks.
- **Tournaments** — open qualifiers, league stages, invitationals and finals.
- **Rankings** — power rankings and circuit standings across African regions.
- **Interviews** — long-form conversations with players, coaches and organisers.
- **Insights** — analysis of the ecosystem, formats, viewership and growth.

### How we work

Every story we publish is checked against a primary source: an official tournament page, a team announcement, a direct statement, or a verified organiser. When something is unconfirmed, we say so — clearly.

> Our standard is simple: if we would not stake our own reputation on it, we do not publish it.

### Follow the network

We publish across every platform the community actually uses:

- **WhatsApp Channel** — instant breaking news alerts.
- **X** — live updates during match days.
- **TikTok** — highlights, clips and quick analysis.
- **Instagram** — graphics, brackets and roster cards.

### Get involved

Are you a tournament organiser, team manager, or player with a story? We want to hear from you. Reach out on any of our social channels and a member of the LAN editorial team will respond.

The African Free Fire scene is growing fast. We are here to document every step of it.

**Welcome to LEAD ACCESS Network.**
